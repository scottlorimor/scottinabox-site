# Mission — scottinabox-site

Personal web presence for Scott Lorimor. This site is the single place someone goes to understand who Scott is, what he's working on, and how to reach him.

## What this site is

- A personal homepage with bio, interests, and contact links
- A blog for long-form writing (coming)
- A clean, fast, static site that works without JavaScript

## What this site is not

- A product landing page
- A portfolio or resume site
- A social feed
